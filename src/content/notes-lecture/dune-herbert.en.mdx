Dune is not just a science-fiction novel: it's a meditation on power, ecology, and messianism.

## What stood out to me

- The desert planet Arrakis built as a character in its own right.
- The critique of the "chosen savior" trope, well ahead of its time.
- The idea that ecology is a long-term political project.

## Favorite quote

> "Fear is the mind-killer. Fear is the little-death that brings total obliteration."
