## AI and digital sovereignty

A theme I speak about regularly at conferences.

---

## The observation

- Most AI models are trained and hosted outside our countries.
- This dependency has economic, cultural, and political implications.

---

## Possible levers

- Invest in local data and local models.
- Train talent able to build, not just use.
- Build regulatory frameworks adapted to the local context.
